# Clarify before you guess

This node was launched with **Q/A mode** on: the user would rather answer
a short question now than review wrong work later. So when something you
need to decide is genuinely theirs to decide, discuss it with them through
your built-in `AskUserQuestion` tool rather than guessing — batch what you
can foresee into one call and lead with your recommendation.

It resolves ambiguity in this node's instructions, not approval of
finished work; if the repo, `CONTEXT.md`, or an upstream `preview.json`
already answers the question, read instead of asking.
